Leadership

Engineering is about building technology. Leadership is about building people. Throughout my experiences, I've enjoyed creating opportunities, mentoring others, and bringing teams together to accomplish goals bigger than any individual could achieve.

National Society of Black Engineers

Academic Excellence Chair

As Academic Excellence Chair for the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) for 2024-2025 at Northwestern University, I focused on strengthening academic support, encouraging collaboration, and building a stronger engineering community.

One of my favorite initiatives was organizing a collaborative study session that brought together students from NSBE, SHPE, SWE, and WIC. More than 50 students attended, creating an inclusive environment where students could study together, share resources, and build relationships across organizations.

Instead of studying separately, students united to prepare for exams, exchange knowledge, and support one another. This demonstrated how collaboration—not competition—creates stronger engineering environments.

Through intentional outreach, event planning, and social media engagement, I also helped increase NSBE meeting attendance by more than 60 students, strengthening chapter involvement overall.

🔬 Research Leadership

Team Lead — Syncytial Knot Isolation Project

Leadership extends beyond student organizations. As the team lead for a microfluidic device design project, I coordinated project planning, delegated responsibilities, and kept our team aligned throughout the engineering design process.

Our goal was to develop a novel microfluidic platform capable of isolating syncytial knots from placental tissue, a challenge with important applications in maternal and fetal health research. Leading this project required balancing technical problem-solving with communication, project management, and collaboration.

From brainstorming design concepts and creating CAD models to planning experiments and presenting our work, I learned that successful engineering depends just as much on teamwork and communication as it does on technical expertise.

🧪 Engineering Outreach

Making Engineering Accessible

I'm passionate about inspiring the next generation of engineers. I have mentored elementary and middle school students in coding and engineering, helping them build confidence through hands-on projects and collaborative problem-solving. Through outreach activities and educational initiatives, I strive to make STEM accessible, engaging, and fun for every student.

Whether mentoring students, creating demonstrations, or collaborating on educational projects, I believe engineering becomes more powerful when knowledge is shared and opportunities are made accessible to everyone.
