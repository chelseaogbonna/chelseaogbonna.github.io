🌍 Project CURE

Global Health Engineering Intern

Objective

Support healthcare systems in areas with limited resources by inspecting, organizing, and preparing donated medical equipment for distribution to hospitals and clinics around the world.

Impact

Project CURE reinforced the importance of designing healthcare technologies that remain effective beyond the laboratory. Seeing the types of equipment requested by hospitals around the world and helping over 10+ countries have strengthened my understanding of global healthcare disparities and the role engineers play in improving access to quality medical care. The experience also emphasized that innovation is most impactful when it is accessible, sustainable, and responsive to the needs of the communities it serves.

My Contributions & Technical Skills

  • Inspected donated medical equipment for functionality and usability Medical Equipment
  • Organized supplies according to hospital and clinical needs Inventory Management
  • Assisted with preparing international medical shipments Global Health Logistics
  • Collaborated with volunteers to improve workflow efficiency Teamwork
  • Learned how donated equipment supports healthcare systems worldwide Healthcare Systems
